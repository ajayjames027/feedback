This comprehensive analytical audit synthesizes structured feedback collected from faculty members regarding the 2025-26 Curricular Design and Development across 33 academic programmes in St. Joseph's College (Autonomous).

The evaluation framework rigorously appraises institutional compliance with Outcome-Based Education (OBE), Bloom's Revised Taxonomy, UGC Learning Outcomes-based Curriculum Framework (LOCF), and National Education Policy (NEP 2020) mandates. Data collected serves as official institutional evidence for Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) scrutiny, statutory Boards of Studies (BoS) revisions, and NAAC Criterion 1 documentation.

2 Audit Scope, Methodology & Participation Demographics

NAAC Criterion 1.4

Statutory Methodology & Metric Scale

The audit utilizes a 5-Point Likert Evaluation Metric (5 = Strongly Agree / Highly Effective / Very Adequate; 4 = Agree / Effective / Adequate; 3 = Neutral / Moderately Effective; 2 = Disagree / Less Effective; 1 = Strongly Disagree / Inadequate).

Attainment percentage is computed as: Attainment % = (Mean Score / 5.00) × 100.

NAAC Quality Attainment Bands

≥ 90% (4.50–5.00): Exemplary
80–89% (4.00–4.49): Commendable
70–79% (3.50–3.99): Satisfactory
60–69% (3.00–3.49): Moderate
< 60% (< 3.00): Critical / Requires Intervention

9 IQAC Strategic Directives & Action Taken Mandates for Boards of Studies

Curricular Action Plan

Pursuant to Section 1.4 of NAAC guidelines and autonomous governance standards of St. Joseph's College (Autonomous), the following strategic directives are officially issued to all Department Chairpersons and Boards of Studies (BoS):

1
Mandatory Integration of Emerging Technologies & Disciplinary Advancements

Every Board of Studies must audit course syllabi against emerging industry trends (specifically Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Green Technologies, and Cyber Ethics) and ensure minimum 20% syllabus renewal in courses operating beyond the 3-year threshold.

2
Elimination of Curricular Overlaps & Unnecessary Duplication

Departments must examine the flagged course entries in Section 5 of this audit report. Syllabi units overlapping with preceding semesters or allied discipline courses must be rationalized and replaced with higher-order cognitive competencies.

3
Strict Enforcement of 5-Year Currency for Textbooks & Reference Literature

In accordance with NAAC quality metrics, reference books and reading lists must be rigorously updated. Classic foundational treatises may be preserved, but each course must incorporate at least two reference publications or online peer-reviewed resources published within the last 5 years.

4
Formal OBE Attainment Loop Closure

Course Outcome (CO) attainment targets and Programme Outcome (PO) mapping matrices must be reviewed annually. Course teachers must document specific syllabus or pedagogical modifications initiated as a direct consequence of low-attainment indicators.

5
Action Taken Report (ATR) Submission Mandate

All Boards of Studies must table this Teacher Feedback Audit Report as a statutory agenda item. An Action Taken Report (ATR) signed by the BoS Chairperson must be submitted to the IQAC and Academic Council within 30 days of the BoS convening.
